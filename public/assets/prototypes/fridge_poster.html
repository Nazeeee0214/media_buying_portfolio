The Fridge Poster

Kitchen height, for everyone in the house, including the babysitter's glance and your own 2am brain.

SAY THIS
“You're safe. I'm staying.” “This feels like a lot. Let's make it smaller.” “Help, space, or a squeeze?” “No talking needed.”
NOT THIS
“Calm down” “You're fine” “Use your words” “Look at me” “If you don't stop...”
DO THIS
  1. Lights down.
  2. Sound off.
  3. People out.
  4. Sit level, to the side.
  5. Breathe out longer than in.
  6. Reconnect before any review.
